Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

WorldNotes is a hobby project that lets you keep notes about countries on a 3D globe. This page explains what it stores and where.

Without signing in

Everything you create (profiles, notes, colors, groups and the theme) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data or using Factory reset deletes it.

The page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical data such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

With cloud sync

Cloud sync is optional and off until you sign in, either with Google or with a one-time link sent to your email.

Google user data

When you choose Continue with Google, WorldNotes asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes openid, email and profile. From these it receives:

  • Email address: identifies your account and is shown in WorldNotes so you know which account you are signed in with.
  • Name and profile picture: received as part of the basic profile; WorldNotes does not display or use them for anything else.
  • Google account ID: links your Google sign-in to your WorldNotes account.

WorldNotes does not access your Gmail, contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data, and never posts anything on your behalf.

WorldNotes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to sign you in and keep your notes in sync. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties except the database provider described below, and not read by people unless you ask for help with your account.

Discord user data

When you choose Continue with Discord, WorldNotes asks Discord only for the identify and email scopes. From these it receives:

  • Email address: identifies your account and is shown in WorldNotes so you know which account you are signed in with.
  • Username and avatar: received as part of your Discord profile; WorldNotes does not display or use them for anything else.
  • Discord user ID: links your Discord sign-in to your WorldNotes account.

WorldNotes does not read your servers, messages or friends list, does not join servers for you and never posts anything on your behalf. Discord user data is used only to sign you in and keep your notes in sync, and is not sold, used for advertising or shared beyond the database provider described below.

What is stored in the cloud

  • Your account: the email address, your Google account ID (if you sign in with Google) and sign-in timestamps, kept by the authentication service.
  • Your WorldNotes data: the same profiles, notes, colors, groups and deletion markers that are in your browser, so your devices can stay in step.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Cloud data is kept by Supabase, the database and sign-in service WorldNotes uses, which processes it on WorldNotes' behalf. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and database rules (row level security) allow each signed-in person to read and change only their own data. WorldNotes has no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.

How long it is kept

Cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and your stored WorldNotes data are removed immediately; copies may remain in Supabase's encrypted backups for a limited time until those backups expire. Signing out keeps the cloud copy so you can sign in again later.

Deleting your data

In WorldNotes open Settings → Cloud sync → Delete account and cloud data. This permanently removes your account and everything stored in the cloud. Data in your browser stays until you clear it yourself or use Factory reset. You can export everything first with Settings → Export notes.

You can also remove WorldNotes' access at any time in your Google account settings.

Children

WorldNotes is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue in the WorldNotes repository.
