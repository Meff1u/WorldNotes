Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

WorldNotes is a hobby project that lets you keep notes about countries on a 3D globe. This page explains what it stores and where.

Without signing in

Everything you create (profiles, notes, colors, groups and the theme) is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data or using Factory reset deletes it.

The page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical data such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

With cloud sync

Cloud sync is optional. If you sign in, WorldNotes stores:

  • Your account: email address and, when signing in with Google, the basic profile Google shares (name and picture). It's used only to sign you in.
  • Your WorldNotes data: the same profiles, notes, colors and groups that are in your browser, so your devices can stay in step.

This data is kept by Supabase, the service WorldNotes uses as its database. Database rules make sure only you can read or change your data. It is not shared, sold or used for anything else, and WorldNotes uses no analytics or ads.

Deleting your data

In WorldNotes open Settings → Cloud sync → Delete account and cloud data. This permanently removes your account and everything stored in the cloud. Data in your browser stays until you clear it yourself.

Signing out stops syncing but keeps both the cloud copy and the data on your device.
